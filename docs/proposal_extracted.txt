1. Introduction
1.1 Project Background
Hospitals manage large volumes of patient information, appointments, prescriptions, laboratory tests, medicine inventories, and financial transactions every day. Many healthcare institutions still rely on manual record-keeping systems or disconnected software applications, which often result in delays, errors, duplicate data entry, and communication difficulties between departments.
These challenges can negatively affect patient care, staff productivity, and overall hospital efficiency. Long waiting times, misplaced records, inventory shortages, and billing inaccuracies are common issues that can arise from inefficient hospital management practices.
PulseOS is proposed as a web-based Hospital Management System that centralizes key hospital operations into a single platform. The system aims to streamline processes, improve communication, reduce paperwork, and provide secure access to healthcare information.
1.2 Project Objectives
The main objectives of PulseOS are:
To digitize hospital administrative and operational activities.
To maintain accurate and centralized patient records.
To improve doctor and staff management.
To streamline appointment scheduling processes.
To manage pharmacy inventory efficiently.
To automate billing and payment operations.
To simplify laboratory test management.
To improve communication among hospital departments.
To enhance data security and confidentiality.
1.3 Target Users
PulseOS is designed for multiple hospital stakeholders, including System Administrators, Doctors, Nurses, Receptionists, Pharmacists, Laboratory Technicians, and Patients. Each user is provided with role-based access permissions to ensure secure and efficient use of the system. A detailed description of each user role is provided in Section 5 (Major Stakeholders / Users).
1.4 Scope
PulseOS covers major hospital operations including patient management, doctor management, appointment scheduling, pharmacy and inventory management, billing and payment processing, laboratory test management, and secure user authentication. The system provides a centralized platform that allows different hospital departments to access and share information efficiently.
For a complete list of system limitations and constraints, refer to Section 8.

6. Six Major Functions
6.1 Patient Management
Module Owner: Somarathna P.C.D.S – IT25103819
Description
The Patient Management module serves as the central repository for storing and managing patient information throughout the patient’s interaction with the hospital.
Who Will Use It?
Receptionist, Doctor, Nurse, Patient, System Administrator
What It Does
This module registers patients, maintains medical records, stores health-related information, and provides authorized users with access to patient data.
CRUD Operations
Create: Creates new patient profiles and medical records for newly registered patients.
Read: Retrieves patient personal information, medical histories, diagnoses, and treatment records.
Update: Updates patient details, contact information, medical histories, and treatment information.
Delete: Archives or removes inactive, duplicate, or invalid patient records when required.
Expected Outcome
Provides accurate and centralized patient information, reducing paperwork and improving the quality of patient care.

8. System Limitations and Constraints
Although PulseOS is designed to improve hospital operations significantly, several limitations and constraints must be considered.
Internet Dependency
Since PulseOS is a web-based application, users must have a stable internet connection to access system functionalities. Network failures may temporarily interrupt access to hospital information.
User Training Requirement
Hospital staff may require training to understand the system features, workflows, and security practices. Improper usage may reduce the effectiveness of the system.
Data Accuracy
The accuracy of reports and records generated by the system depends on the quality of data entered by users. Incorrect or incomplete information may affect decision-making and patient care.
Infrastructure Requirement
The hospital must maintain suitable servers, computer systems, and network resources to ensure smooth operation and acceptable performance.
Integration Constraints
The initial version of PulseOS will not support integration with external healthcare systems, insurance services, or government medical databases.
Security Risks
Although security controls will be implemented, regular system updates, monitoring, and maintenance are required to protect against cybersecurity threats.
Maintenance Requirement
The system requires regular software updates, database backups, and technical support to maintain reliability and prevent data loss.
Browser-Based Operation
The system is designed primarily for web browser access and does not include dedicated mobile or desktop applications in the first version.

10.4 Execution Strategy
To ensure the successful and timely completion of PulseOS, the team will adopt an Agile/Scrum development methodology. The project will be executed through a series of weekly sprints, each with clearly defined goals aligned to the project timeline presented in Section 9. At the beginning of each sprint, the team will conduct a sprint planning session to prioritize tasks, assign responsibilities, and set deliverables for the week.
Each of the six team members is assigned ownership of one major functional module, ensuring equitable workload distribution and individual accountability. Daily stand-up meetings (or a minimum of three per week) will be held to discuss progress, address blockers, and coordinate cross-module dependencies. Sprint review and retrospective meetings will be conducted at the end of each sprint to evaluate completed work and identify areas for improvement.
Progress will be tracked using a shared task management tool, and version control (Git) will be used for collaborative code management. Continuous integration and testing practices will be implemented from Week 7 onward to detect issues early and maintain code quality throughout the development lifecycle.
10.5 Closing Remarks
PulseOS is expected to provide a practical and reliable solution for modernizing hospital operations. By integrating patient management, appointment scheduling, pharmacy services, billing functions, and laboratory operations into one platform, the system will support better healthcare delivery and improved hospital performance.
